Scrapyard SFX Brief

Every sound the game needs, with the prompt that makes it and the filename it becomes. The definitions live in sfx-set.js — this page, the generator and the picker all read that one file, so a prompt cannot be changed in one place and generated from another.

Generating. Put your key in .env as ELEVENLABS_API_KEY=sk_... (gitignored), then node sfx/generate.mjs — four takes of each into sfx/takes/. Open picker.html to choose between them. Existing takes are skipped, so it is safe to re-run.
Against Vampire Survivors. That teardown counted 222 clips; this is —. Three of its five patterns are taken wholesale — explosions are graded rather than sourced, impact follows the damage type rather than the weapon, and the reward loop is covered more densely than the fighting. Two are declined for now: A/B pairs on repeated clips (the rows marked repeats are where that spend goes first) and phrased set pieces, where a boss gets a sentence rather than a sound.
Filenames are the catalog keys. When the picked files land in public/sfx/ they get wired into src/render/audio/sfxCatalog.ts against these exact names — anything the catalog names but disk lacks is then a missing sound rather than a silent one.
